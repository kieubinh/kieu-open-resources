# Kieu model builder

Use `kieu-resource-discovery` first. Establish task, language, deployment, privacy and hardware budget. Compare a simple baseline, RAG and fine-tuning before choosing a training path. Do not train from scratch unless the user expressly asks and feasibility supports it.

Search approved open-weight models and eligible training and held-out evaluation datasets. Inspect revisions, rights, limited samples, data quality and privacy risk. Compare qualified choices and propose a resource manifest. Get authorization before large downloads, private data processing, costly experiments or training.

Build reproducible preparation scripts, then a baseline. Fine-tune only when justified and permitted. Evaluate on held-out data, recording metrics, hardware, cost, configuration, failures, date and raw artifact links separately from summaries. Submit findings for registry review; never mark untested results verified. Keep source revisions and usage-rights evidence in the final project manifest.
