# Kieu Learning English

Create a clear, exam-informed long-form TOEIC practice episode for the Maya & Emma English series. Keep the episode focused on TOEIC; do not repurpose unrelated older channel videos as TOEIC lessons.

## Series format

- Make long-form episodes only. Do not create Shorts for this series unless the user explicitly asks to change this preference.
- Label episodes in sequence (`EP06`, `EP07`, …) and show the TOEIC section, Part, and topic in the opening, thumbnail, title, and video description.
- Assign each episode a specific workplace or everyday professional topic and one TOEIC question type. Keep the order easy to follow across the long-form TOEIC playlist.
- Check the current official TOEIC format and recent official or authorized practice material before writing. Use those sources to model task shape, timing, and difficulty; write original questions, options, passages, answers, and explanations rather than reproducing copyrighted test material.
- Use Maya and Emma’s established voices and visual style when available. Keep test prompts at natural exam speed; make answer explanations slower and clear, and subtitle the explanations.

## Listening exam and review segments

Build every TOEIC Listening question in two distinct stages:

1. **Attempt:** play the test audio at exam speed. Do not show the spoken transcript, captions, or subtitles while the learner is answering. Display only the material the real Part provides on the page: for Parts 3 and 4, the written questions and choices may appear; never display the conversation or talk transcript. Part 2 has no printed question or response options, so keep those audio-only during the attempt.
2. **Review:** replay the relevant audio with synchronized transcript subtitles visible. After the replay, show the correct answer and explain the listening clue and, when useful, why distractors do not fit. Keep explanation captions synchronized with Maya or Emma’s slower explanation.

For Reading episodes, show the passage, question, and answer choices during the attempt because those are the test material. Reveal the answer and explanation in the review stage.

## Media quality checks

Before release, inspect the rendered attempt segment for any leaked listening transcript or captions. Confirm that transcript subtitles begin only with the review/replay, that the answer is not revealed early, and that the explanation captions match the spoken audio. Check the exported video’s resolution, duration, audio presence, subtitle timing, and thumbnail. Fix any mismatch before proceeding.

## Release

Finish and check one long episode at a time, then add it to the long-form TOEIC playlist. Keep the Shorts playlist separate and unchanged unless the user asks for Shorts. Follow any action-time platform confirmation required for public submission.
